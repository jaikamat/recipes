### Banana Oat Muffins

**Servings:** 12 muffins
**Per serving:** 172 cal | 3g protein | 8g fat | 24g carbs

**Ingredients:**

_Dry:_
- 120g oat flour (blended rolled oats)
- 80g (1 cup) rolled oats
- 7g (1 TBSP) flax seeds (optional)
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp cinnamon

_Wet:_
- 100g (1 medium) ripe banana, mashed
- 100g applesauce
- 160g (120ml) maple syrup
- 72g (80ml) melted ghee or olive oil
- 120ml milk
- 1 tsp vanilla

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 350°F
2. Mix oat flour, rolled oats, flax seeds, baking soda, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon in a large bowl
3. In a separate bowl, whisk mashed banana, applesauce, maple syrup, melted ghee, milk, and vanilla until combined
4. Pour wet ingredients into dry and fold gently until just combined — do not overmix
5. Scoop batter into 12 lined or greased muffin cups
6. Bake for 18-22 minutes, until lightly golden and a toothpick comes out clean

**Freeze:** Cool completely, then store in a freezer bag for up to 3 months.

**Reheat:** From frozen, microwave for 30-45 seconds.

**Notes:** Let muffins cool fully in the pan before removing — gluten-free crumb is more fragile when warm. Fold in blueberries, walnuts, or chocolate chips before scooping if desired — adjust macros accordingly.
