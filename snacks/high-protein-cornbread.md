### High-Protein Cornbread

**Servings:** 8 wedges
**Per serving:** 183 cal | 8g protein | 5g fat | 25g carbs

**Ingredients:**

- 240g (2 cups) Martha White self-rising cornmeal, plus 1/2 tsp for skillet
- 30-40g unflavored whey protein powder
- 360ml (~1.5 cups) whole milk, adjust for consistency
- 1 large egg
- 1 TBSP neutral oil, plus 1-2 tsp for skillet

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 450°F and place a 10-inch cast iron skillet inside while preheating
2. Whisk together cornmeal and whey protein in a large bowl
3. In a separate bowl, whisk egg and milk together
4. Pour wet ingredients into dry and stir until combined. Aim for pancake batter consistency - pourable but not watery. Add milk gradually if needed as whey will thicken the batter
5. Heat 1 TBSP oil until very hot (30 seconds in microwave) and stir into batter
6. Carefully remove hot skillet from oven, add 1-2 tsp oil and swirl to coat. Sprinkle 1/2 tsp cornmeal over oil and let toast until just brown (~30 seconds)
7. Pour batter into hot skillet and bake 20 minutes until top is golden brown and edges pull away from pan
8. Flip onto plate and cut into 8 wedges

**Freeze:** Wrap individual wedges in plastic wrap and store in freezer bag.

**Reheat:** Microwave for 1-2 minutes or air fryer at 350°F for 5 minutes.

**Serve:** Hot with butter, honey, or alongside chili.
