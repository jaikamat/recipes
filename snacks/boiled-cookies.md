### Boiled Cookies

**Servings:** 36 cookies
**Per serving:** 111 cal | 2g protein | 5g fat | 16g carbs
**Per 100g:** 396 cal | 7g protein | 18g fat | 57g carbs (~28g per cookie)

Classic no-bake chocolate peanut butter oat cookies — just boil, mix, and drop.

**Ingredients:**

- 400g (2 cups) granulated sugar
- 120ml (1/2 cup) Fairlife 2% ultra-filtered milk
- 113g (1 stick) butter
- 21g (1/4 cup) dutch process cocoa powder
- 200g (2 1/2 cups) rolled oats
- 130g (1/2 cup) peanut butter
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 110g (1 cup) chopped pecans (optional)

**Instructions:**

1. Combine sugar, milk, butter, and baking cocoa in a medium saucepan
2. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly. Boil for 1 1/2 minutes
3. Remove from heat and stir in oats, peanut butter, vanilla, and pecans (if using)
4. Drop by teaspoonfuls onto waxed paper or a parchment-lined baking sheet
5. Let stand at room temperature until cool and set, about 30 minutes

**Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week.

**Notes:** Pecans are optional — the cookies are great without them. Adding pecans adds roughly 19 cal and 2g fat per cookie.
