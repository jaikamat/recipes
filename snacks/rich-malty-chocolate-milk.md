### Rich Malty Chocolate Milk

**Servings:** 2
**Per serving:** 337 cal | 43g carbs | 11g protein | 15g fat

A rich, warming chocolate milk made in the Vitamix. The oats blend completely smooth and the friction heat melts the chocolate chips, creating a creamy, malty drink.

**Ingredients:**

- 480ml (2 cups) Lactaid milk
- 2 tbsp dutch process cocoa powder
- 42g (1/4 cup) semi-sweet chocolate chips (Toll House)
- 2 tbsp rolled oats
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp molasses
- 1 pinch salt
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

**Instructions:**

1. Add all ingredients to a Vitamix blender.
2. Start on speed 1 for a few seconds to pull everything down into the blades, then ramp up to speed 10.
3. Blend for 4-5 minutes — this gives the oats time to fully break down and generates enough heat to melt the chocolate chips and marry everything together. The container will feel warm.
4. Taste and adjust sweetness if needed.

**Storage:** Refrigerate in a sealed container for up to 2 days. Shake or stir before serving — some settling is normal. Not recommended for freezing as the texture will separate.

**Notes:** The molasses and brown sugar together are fairly sweet — you may want to reduce one or the other to taste. The extended blend time is essential for smooth texture and proper heat.
