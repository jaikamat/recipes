### Saffron Chai

**Servings:** 2
**Per serving:** 126 cal | 19g carbs | 4g protein | 4g fat

A spiced Assam chai with ginger paste, warm spices, and a touch of saffron.

**Ingredients:**

- 830ml (3.5 cups) water
- 2 tsp ginger paste
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 4 whole cloves
- 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
- 1 pinch ground black pepper
- 2 saffron strands
- 4 tsp Assam tea
- 240ml (1 cup) whole milk
- 23g (0.8 oz) brown sugar

**Instructions:**

1. Combine water, ginger paste, cinnamon stick, cloves, ground cardamom, black pepper, and saffron strands in a pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer for 10 minutes.
2. Add Assam tea and simmer for 3-5 minutes until strong and malty.
3. Add milk and brown sugar. Stir to dissolve the sugar. Bring back up to a rolling boil — watch the pot closely as it will foam up quickly.
4. As soon as the chai rises to a rolling boil, cut the heat. Optionally, bring it back to a boil 2-3 more times for a creamier result.
5. Strain through a fine mesh strainer into cups. Serve immediately.

**Notes:** Ginger paste is more concentrated than fresh — start with 1 tsp if you prefer milder ginger. The black pepper is to taste; a few cracks from a pepper mill is a good starting point. The saffron adds a subtle floral note and golden color.
