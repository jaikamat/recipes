### Chocolate Peanut Butter Banana Protein Shake

**Servings:** 1
**Per serving:** 399 cal | 38g protein | 17g fat | 32g carbs
**Per 100g:** 92 cal | 9g protein | 4g fat | 7g carbs (~435g per serving)

A thick, frosty, fully non-dairy chocolate-peanut-butter shake built for a cut — ~38g protein at just under 400 calories. Frozen banana and cocojune give it real milkshake body, while a blend of PBfit powder and a little real peanut butter keeps the roasty PB flavor rich without the fat hit. Made in a Vitamix.

**Ingredients:**

- 80g (about 1 small) banana, very ripe, mashed and frozen
- 270ml Ripple unsweetened original pea milk
- 30g (2 TBSP) cocojune unsweetened coconut yogurt
- 21g (3/4 scoop) Truvani unflavored protein powder
- 14g (about 2 TBSP) PBfit peanut butter powder
- 10g (about 1.5 tsp) peanut butter
- 10g (2 TBSP) dutch process cocoa powder
- 16g (4 tsp) erythritol, or to taste
- 1/4 tsp decaf espresso powder
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/8 tsp ground cardamom (optional)
- 1/8 tsp xanthan gum
- Pinch of sea salt

**Instructions:**

1. Add the Ripple, frozen banana, cocojune, PBfit powder, peanut butter, cocoa powder, erythritol, espresso powder, vanilla, cardamom (if using), and salt to the Vitamix. Hold back the Truvani protein and xanthan gum for now
2. Start on speed 1 to pull everything into the blades, then ramp to speed 10 and blend until completely smooth and frosty, about 30-45 seconds — don't over-blend or the friction heat will melt the frozen banana and thin it out
3. Add the Truvani protein and xanthan gum and blend on low just until incorporated, a few seconds — adding them after the rest is smooth keeps the shake from whipping in air and turning foamy or gluey
4. Taste and adjust — a touch more erythritol if you want it sweeter, a splash of Ripple if it's thicker than you like
5. Pour and drink immediately while it's cold and thick

**Serve:** Best straight from the blender. A pinch of flaky salt or a dusting of cocoa on top sharpens the first sip.

**Storage:** Meant to be fresh — the frozen banana and xanthan set up and the texture separates on standing. If you must hold it, refrigerate up to 1 day and re-blend or shake hard before drinking.

**Notes:**

- This uses 3/4 scoop (21g) of Truvani to land just under 400 cal. For a higher-protein version (~425 cal, 43g protein), use a full 28g scoop — the cleanest way to add protein without touching anything else
- To go leaner still (~375 cal), drop the real peanut butter to 6g and keep the PBfit — you lose a little richness but the roasty flavor holds
- For a richer, more indulgent version, swap the erythritol for 1 pitted Medjool date (adds ~65 cal of caramel depth) or bump the real peanut butter to 16g
- Everything in this shake is unsweetened — the cocoa is bitter, the cocojune is tangy, and the pea protein is earthy — so it needs more sweetener than a shake built on sweetened powders or milk. 16g (4 tsp) is a lightly-sweet baseline; taste and push toward 20g if you want it dessert-sweet
- Sweetener note: this assumes an erythritol that's 1:1 with sugar by volume (an erythritol + monk fruit/stevia blend, like Lakanto). Plain erythritol is only ~70% as sweet, so use roughly 30% more (≈20g) to match
- The 1/8 tsp ground cardamom takes this in a floral, lightly chai-like direction — it plays off the banana, coconut yogurt, and espresso. Keep it to a small pinch; cardamom turns soapy fast if overdone. Swap in 1/4 tsp cinnamon instead for cozier warmth, or a tiny pinch of cayenne for a Mexican-hot-chocolate edge
- Keep the xanthan light (1/8 tsp) — frozen banana and cocojune already thicken it, and too much turns it slimy
- No frozen banana? Use cold mashed banana plus 3-4 ice cubes and blend a few seconds longer to build body
