### Blackberry Basil Lemonade

**Servings:** 2
**Per serving:** 135 cal | 1g protein | 0g fat | 34g carbs
**Per 100g:** 52 cal | 0g protein | 0g fat | 13g carbs (~260g per serving)

Everything goes in the blender, then through a strainer. Adapted from the [Allrecipes Blackberry Lemonade](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/280577/homemade-blackberry-lemonade/).

**Ingredients:**

- 150g (1 cup) fresh blackberries
- 50g (1/4 cup) granulated sugar
- 1 lemon, juiced (~45ml)
- 6-8 basil leaves (picked from stems)
- 360ml (1.5 cups) cold water

**Instructions:**

1. Add blackberries, sugar, lemon juice, basil leaves, and water to a blender
2. Blend until smooth, about 30 seconds
3. Pour through a fine-mesh strainer into a pitcher, pressing with a spoon to extract all the liquid. Discard solids
4. Taste and adjust sweetness — add more sugar if too tart or a splash of water if too sweet
5. Serve over ice

**Notes:** Use basil leaves only — stems can add bitterness even when blended. If basil leaves are large, 4-5 is plenty.
