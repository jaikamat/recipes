### No-Bake Protein Bars

**Servings:** 12
**Per serving:** 345 cal | 18g protein | 21g fat | 23g carbs
**Per 100g:** 426 cal | 22g protein | 26g fat | 28g carbs (~81g per bar)

**Ingredients:**

- 100g medjool dates, roughly chopped
- 250g crunchy peanut butter
- 125g vanilla whey powder
- 100g ground almonds (or almond flour)
- 75g chia seeds, milled
- 2 TBSP maple syrup
- ~150ml water
- 150g chocolate chips, roughly chopped

_Optional toppings:_

- chopped pistachios
- dried goji berries
- coconut flakes
- cacao nibs

**Instructions:**

1. Boil water and soak dates in a bowl for 10 minutes, then mash with a fork until spreadable
2. In a food processor, blend peanut butter, protein powder, ground almonds, milled chia seeds, maple syrup, and water until smooth
3. Divide the mixture evenly into two 6-count 1/2 cup silicone molds, pressing down firmly
4. Spread mashed dates evenly on top of each portion
5. Melt dark chocolate in a bowl over simmering water (don't let the bowl touch the water)
6. Pour melted chocolate over each bar and spread to cover
7. Sprinkle with desired toppings while chocolate is still wet
8. Refrigerate for 30 minutes until firm

**Freeze:** In 1/2 cup molds overnight after completely set.

**Reheat:** Thaw in refrigerator or enjoy cold.

**Serve:** Keeps up to 2 weeks in the fridge.
