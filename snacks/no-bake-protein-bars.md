### No-Bake Protein Bars

**Servings:** 12
**Per serving:** 354 cal | 22g protein | 20g fat | 26g carbs
**Per 100g:** 379 cal | 23g protein | 22g fat | 28g carbs (~93g per bar)

Dairy-free peanut butter bars with chopped dark chocolate folded through, built on a blend of vanilla and unflavored plant protein, with salt, vanilla, and cinnamon dialed in to mask the earthiness of pea protein.

**Ingredients:**

- 300g crunchy peanut butter
- 125g truvani unflavored protein powder
- 75g orgain vanilla protein powder
- 100g peanut butter powder
- 100g medjool dates, roughly chopped
- 60g (3 TBSP) maple syrup
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- ~200ml water
- 150g dark chocolate (70% cacao), roughly chopped

**Instructions:**

1. Boil water and soak dates in a bowl for 10 minutes, then drain
2. In a food processor, blend the soaked dates, peanut butter, both protein powders, peanut butter powder, maple syrup, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and sea salt, streaming in water until it comes together as a thick, fudgy dough
3. Taste the base and adjust — it should taste slightly sweeter and saltier than you want the finished bar since chilling mutes both; add maple syrup, salt, or vanilla until no beany edge remains
4. Rest the dough for 10 minutes so the pea protein fully hydrates and cools — warm dough will smear the chocolate
5. Fold the chopped dark chocolate through the dough by hand until evenly distributed, then divide evenly into two 6-count 1/2 cup silicone molds, pressing down firmly
6. Sprinkle with flaky sea salt — and chopped roasted peanuts, pistachios, or coconut flakes if you like — pressing lightly so it sticks
7. Refrigerate for 30 minutes until firm

**Freeze:** In 1/2 cup molds overnight after completely set.

**Reheat:** Thaw in refrigerator or enjoy cold.

**Storage:** Keeps up to 2 weeks in the fridge.

**Notes:** Flavor and texture improve after an overnight rest in the fridge as the pea protein finishes hydrating. The dates are blended into the base to sweeten it where the pea protein is; folded through the sweet base, the 70% chocolate reads less bitter than it would as a solid top coat. Don't process the chocolate with the dough — the blade smears it instead of leaving distinct pieces. Dairy-free dark chocolate chips can be folded in whole instead of chopping. The powder blend is deliberate — all Orgain caps protein around 16g per bar, while all pea isolate tastes noticeably earthier. No peanut butter powder? Substitute 100g almond flour toasted in a dry pan, dropping ~3g protein per bar.
