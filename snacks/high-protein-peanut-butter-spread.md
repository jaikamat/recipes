### High Protein Peanut Butter Spread

**Servings:** 1
**Per serving:** 170 cal | 23g protein | 5g fat | 12g carbs

A quick, high-protein peanut butter spread with a fraction of the fat of regular PB.

**Ingredients:**

- 30g PBfit peanut butter powder
- 10g vanilla whey protein
- 6g erythritol
- 2 TBSP water
- Pinch of sea salt

**Instructions:**

1. Combine peanut butter powder, whey protein, erythritol, and salt in a small bowl
2. Add water and stir until smooth and spreadable

**Notes:** Adjust water a teaspoon at a time for desired consistency. Works well on toast, rice cakes, or as a dip for fruit.
