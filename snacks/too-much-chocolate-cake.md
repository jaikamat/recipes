### Too Much Chocolate Cake

**Servings:** 12
**Per serving:** 675 cal | 77g carbs | 5g protein | 41g fat

Devil's food cake loaded with pudding mix, sour cream, and chocolate chips, topped with pourable chocolate icing. Based on the award-winning Allrecipes recipe with Mom's tweaks.

**Ingredients:**

_Cake:_
- 432g (15.25 oz) box devil's food cake mix
- 167g (5.9 oz) box instant chocolate pudding mix
- 230g (1 cup) sour cream
- 218ml (1 cup) vegetable oil
- 180ml (3/4 cup) warm water
- 3 large eggs
- 336g (2 cups) semisweet chocolate chips

_Topping:_
- 1 can (16 oz) chocolate icing

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat oven to 350°F and generously grease a 12-cup Bundt pan
2. Beat cake mix, pudding mix, sour cream, oil, water, and eggs in a large bowl until smooth and well blended
3. Stir in chocolate chips
4. Pour batter into the prepared Bundt pan
5. Bake for 50-55 minutes, until the top is springy to the touch and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean
6. Cool cake thoroughly in the pan for at least 1.5 hours before inverting onto a plate
7. Microwave the can of chocolate icing for 30 seconds to melt, then pour evenly over the cake

**Storage:** Airtight container at room temperature for up to several days. The cake stays very moist.

**Notes:** Mom uses 3 eggs and a bit more water for a thinner batter — 4 eggs (the original amount) also thins it out enough if you prefer. A 13x9" pan works in place of the Bundt pan.
