### Soy-Free Mapo Tofu

**Servings:** 2
**Per serving:** 474 cal | 42g protein | 23g fat | 22g carbs
**Per 100g:** 91 cal | 8g protein | 4g fat | 4g carbs (~520g per serving)

A soy- and dairy-free take on the canonical Mapo Tofu in dinner/ — Egg White Tofu stands in for the soft tofu, coconut aminos for the soy sauce, and olive oil for the ghee. Uses a soy-free doubanjiang (fava-bean-only; check the label, many brands add soybeans).

**Ingredients:**

- 2/3 batch Egg White Tofu (see recipe; ~400g), cut into 1-inch cubes
- 30ml (2 TBSP) olive oil
- 125g ground turkey (93/7)
- 40g doubanjiang (soy-free brand; fermented bean & chile paste)
- 15g gochugaru chile powder
- 15g ginger paste
- 30g garlic paste
- 75g scallions, sliced (white and light green parts separated)

- 2g Szechuan peppercorns, ground
- 30g Shaoxing wine
- 300g (1.25 cups) Kirkland chicken bone broth
- 15g coconut aminos
- 3g sea salt
- 1g MSG
- 5g cornstarch, mixed with 20g water
- 5g sesame oil

**Instructions:**

1. Heat olive oil in a large pan or pot over medium-high heat. Add ground turkey and cook, breaking it up, until browned
2. Add doubanjiang and gochugaru. Stir and cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant and oil turns red
3. Add ginger, garlic, and white parts of scallions. Cook for another minute
4. Pour in Shaoxing wine and let it sizzle for 30 seconds
5. Add bone broth, coconut aminos, salt, and MSG. Bring to a simmer
6. Stir cornstarch slurry and drizzle into the pot. Gently stir until sauce thickens, about 1 minute
7. Drop the heat to low and gently fold in the egg white tofu cubes. Warm through for 2-3 minutes without a hard boil — the cubes are more delicate than soy tofu and just need heating, not simmering
8. Remove from heat. Drizzle with sesame oil and top with Szechuan peppercorns and green parts of scallions

**Freeze:** Not recommended with the egg white tofu — egg gels weep and turn spongy when frozen. Freeze the sauce alone and fold in fresh cubes on reheat.

**Reheat:** Warm gently in a pot over medium-low heat. Add a splash of broth if too thick.

**Notes:** Versus the canonical recipe: the sauce is thickened before the tofu goes in (not after) so the cubes get folded, not stirred through a boil. Coconut aminos are sweeter and less salty than soy sauce, so the canonical recipe's 5g sugar is dropped entirely — the aminos carry enough sweetness on their own. Versus soft tofu, the egg white tofu trades ~65 cal and nearly all of the tofu's fat for ~11g more protein across the batch.
