### Turkey Biryani

**Servings:** 4
**Per serving:** 613 cal | 47g protein | 17g fat | 62g carbs
**Per 100g:** 153 cal | 12g protein | 4g fat | 15g carbs (~400g per serving)

A quick weeknight biryani using ground turkey and a DEEP brand ready-to-cook biryani spice packet. Coconut yogurt keeps it dairy-free and creamy, and spinach sneaks in extra greens without changing the flavor.

**Ingredients:**

_Turkey seasoning:_

- 454g (1 lb) ground turkey (93/7 lean)
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/4 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 10g garlic paste
- Squeeze of lemon juice

_Biryani:_

- 300g (2 Zojirushi cups) uncooked basmati rice
- 465ml (~2 cups) Kirkland chicken bone broth (for rice)
- 100g cocojune unsweetened coconut yogurt
- 1 DEEP brand ready-to-cook biryani spice packet
- 240ml (1 cup) Kirkland chicken bone broth (for sauce)
- 100-150g spinach, chopped (fresh or frozen, thawed and squeezed dry)
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 1 TBSP olive oil

_Garnish:_

- 30g cashews
- Cilantro

_Optional garnish:_

- Fried onions
- Fresh mint

**Instructions:**

1. Mix ground turkey with garam masala, coriander, cumin, salt, ginger, garlic, and lemon juice. Let sit 15-20 minutes while the rice cooks
2. Rinse basmati rice until water runs clear. Add rice and 465ml bone broth to the Zojirushi and cook on the white rice setting. Fluff and set aside
3. Heat oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add seasoned turkey, break it up, and brown well with good color for 5-7 minutes
4. Add diced onion and a pinch of salt. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and sweat for 3-4 minutes until softened
5. Add the entire spice packet, bone broth, coconut yogurt, and chopped spinach. Stir to combine and simmer on medium-low until a thick sauce coats the meat, about 5-8 minutes. Taste for salt
6. Spread cooked rice evenly over the meat layer — do not mix. Add garnishes if using
7. Cover tightly, reduce to lowest heat, and cook 10 minutes until steam escapes the lid
8. Remove from heat and rest covered for 5 minutes, then fold gently to combine the layers

**Freeze:** Cool completely, portion into airtight containers, and freeze for up to 3 months.

**Reheat:** Microwave from frozen 3-4 minutes, stirring halfway. Or thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat in a covered skillet over medium-low with a splash of water to steam through.

**Serve:** Garnish with fried onions, cashews, cilantro, or mint. A side of raita pairs well.

**Notes:** Stir the coconut yogurt in off a hard boil so it stays creamy and doesn't split. If using frozen spinach, squeeze out excess water first or the sauce will thin out. A handful of frozen peas can replace the spinach for variety. Adding fried cashews or onions adds ~50-80 cal per serving; for extra richness, drizzle a little ghee over the rice before steaming (~110 cal per TBSP across the dish).
