### Turkey Biryani

**Servings:** 4
**Per serving:** 566 cal | 60g carbs | 41g protein | 16g fat
**Per 100g:** 162 cal | 17g carbs | 12g protein | 5g fat (~355g per serving)

A quick weeknight biryani using ground turkey and a DEEP brand ready-to-cook biryani spice packet. Cottage cheese stands in for yogurt, and spinach sneaks in extra greens without changing the flavor.

**Ingredients:**

_Turkey seasoning:_

- 300g ground turkey (93/7 lean)
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Squeeze of lemon juice

_Biryani:_

- 300g (2 Zojirushi cups) uncooked basmati rice
- 465ml (~2 cups) Kirkland chicken bone broth (for rice)
- 100g cottage cheese (2% lowfat), blended smooth
- 1 DEEP brand ready-to-cook biryani spice packet
- 240ml (1 cup) Kirkland chicken bone broth (for sauce)
- 100-150g chopped spinach (fresh or frozen, thawed and squeezed dry)
- 1 TBSP oil
- 1 TBSP ghee

_Garnish:_

- 30g cashews
- Fresh cilantro

_Optional garnish:_

- Fried onions
- Fresh mint

**Instructions:**

1. Mix ground turkey with garam masala, coriander, cumin, salt, ginger, garlic, and lemon juice. Let sit 15-20 minutes while the rice cooks
2. Rinse basmati rice until water runs clear. Add rice and 465ml bone broth to the Zojirushi and cook on the white rice setting. Fluff and set aside
3. Heat oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add seasoned turkey, break it up, and brown well with good color for 5-7 minutes
4. Add the entire spice packet, bone broth, blended cottage cheese, and chopped spinach. Stir to combine and simmer on medium-low until a thick sauce coats the meat, about 5-8 minutes. Taste for salt
5. Spread cooked rice evenly over the meat layer — do not mix. Drizzle ghee over the top and add garnishes if using
6. Cover tightly, reduce to lowest heat, and cook 10 minutes until steam escapes the lid
7. Remove from heat and rest covered for 5 minutes, then fold gently to combine the layers

**Freeze:** Cool completely, portion into airtight containers, and freeze for up to 3 months.

**Reheat:** Microwave from frozen 3-4 minutes, stirring halfway. Or thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat in a covered skillet over medium-low with a splash of water to steam through.

**Serve:** Garnish with fried onions, cashews, cilantro, or mint. A side of raita pairs well.

**Notes:** Blend the cottage cheese until completely smooth — unblended curds won't melt into the sauce properly. If using frozen spinach, squeeze out excess water first or the sauce will thin out. A handful of frozen peas can replace the spinach for variety. Going heavier on ghee or adding fried cashews/onions adds ~50-80 cal per serving.
