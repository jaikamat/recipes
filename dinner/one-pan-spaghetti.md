### One-Pan Spaghetti

**Servings:** 4 (690g each)
**Per serving:** 711 cal | 72g protein | 24g fat | 87g carbs
**Per 100g:** 103 cal | 10g protein | 3g fat | 13g carbs (~690g per serving)

**Ingredients:**

- 906g (2 lb) turkey italian sausage (bulk ground, casings removed)
- 340g (12 oz) rotini or pasta of choice
- 794g (28 oz) crushed tomatoes, canned
- 794g (28 oz) chicken bone broth
- 184g (6.5 oz) onion, diced
- 70g tomato paste
- 50g parmesan cheese, shredded
- 15g chicken bouillon paste (Better Than Bouillon)
- 12g (4 cloves) garlic, minced
- 10g fresh basil
- 8g brown sugar
- 0.5 tsp dried oregano
- 0.5 tsp chili flakes
- Salt to taste (6-8g works with Costco broth)
- Oil for cooking

**Instructions:**

1. Heat a dutch oven or large heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat. Add a bit of oil and the ground turkey, breaking the meat apart into small pieces as it cooks
2. When the meat begins to brown and is well broken down, stir in the onion, garlic, and a pinch of salt, scraping up any fond stuck to the bottom. Cover and cook for 5 minutes
3. Preheat your oven to 425°F
4. After 5 minutes, stir in oregano, chili flakes, and tomato paste and cook for a minute until tomato paste is well incorporated
5. Stir in bouillon, crushed tomatoes, broth, salt, and brown sugar and bring to a simmer
6. Stir in basil. Add pasta and gently stir, pressing down to submerge. Simmer for about 60 seconds
7. Stir pasta again to fully submerge. Transfer pot to the oven uncovered for 16-18 minutes, stirring and submerging at 5 and 10 minutes
8. Taste for doneness and seasoning, adjust if necessary. Stir in parmesan and garnish with more if desired

**Freeze:** In 2-cup molds after cooling.

**Reheat:** Place frozen portion into a 2-cup stoneware container with a splash of broth. Tent loosely with foil and bake at 350°F for 60-90 minutes, stirring halfway through once edges loosen. Remove foil and bake 5-10 more minutes until bubbling and excess moisture cooks off.
