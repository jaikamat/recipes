### Taco Pasta Skillet

**Servings:** 4
**Per serving:** 630 cal | 60g protein | 37g fat | 48g carbs
**Per 100g:** 137 cal | 13g protein | 8g fat | 10g carbs (~460g per serving)

**Ingredients:**

- 30ml (2 TBSP) extra virgin olive oil
- 10g garlic paste
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 567g (1.25 lbs) ground turkey (taco seasoned) or ground turkey + low-sodium taco seasoning packet
- 480ml (2 cups) Kirkland chicken bone broth
- 538g (19oz) can red enchilada sauce
- 227g (8oz, about 2.5 cups) Carbe Diem elbow pasta
- 226g (2 cups) cheddar (Kerrygold Reserve)
- 60g (2 cups) spinach
- Pinch of sea salt

**Instructions:**

1. Heat olive oil over medium-high to high heat. Add ground meat in an even layer and let it sit undisturbed for 2-3 minutes to get a hard sear and build fond. Flip, repeat, then break up with a wooden spoon and finish browning. If using unseasoned meat, stir in taco seasoning packet now
2. Add diced onion, garlic paste, and a pinch of salt. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and sweat for 3-4 minutes until onions are soft and translucent, stirring partway through
3. Add pasta, chicken broth, and enchilada sauce to the pan. The broth will deglaze the remaining fond
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and cover
5. Cook covered for about 15 minutes. Add spinach partway through
6. Remove lid and simmer for an additional 5 minutes until pasta is tender and sauce has reduced
7. Remove from heat and stir in the shredded cheddar

**Freeze:** Portion into containers after cooling. Pasta may soften slightly upon reheating.

**Reheat:** Warm in a pan over medium-low heat with a splash of broth to loosen. Microwave works but stovetop gives better texture.
