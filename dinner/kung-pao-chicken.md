### Kung Pao Chicken

**Servings:** 4
**Per serving:** 383 cal | 31g protein | 20g fat | 18g carbs

**Ingredients:**

_Chicken & Marinade:_

- 454g (1 lb) chicken breast, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 15g Shaoxing wine
- 15g light soy sauce
- 6g cornstarch

_Sauce:_

- 15g light soy sauce
- 8g dark soy sauce (optional, for color)
- 30g Chinese black vinegar
- 15g Shaoxing wine
- 25g sugar
- 45g water
- 5g cornstarch
- 5g sesame oil

_Stir-fry:_

- 30g ghee (high smoke point for stir-frying)
- 8-10 dried red chiles, snipped and seeds shaken out
- 2g Szechuan peppercorns
- 10g garlic, minced
- 10g ginger, minced
- 75g scallions, cut into 1-inch pieces (white and green separated)
- 75g roasted peanuts

**Instructions:**

1. Combine chicken with marinade ingredients in a bowl. Set aside for 15-20 minutes
2. Whisk all sauce ingredients together in a small bowl
3. Heat ghee in a large pan or wok over high heat. Add chicken in a single layer and sear until browned, 2-3 minutes per side. Remove and set aside
4. Add more ghee if needed. Fry dried chiles and Szechuan peppercorns for 30 seconds until fragrant (don't let them burn)
5. Add garlic, ginger, and white parts of scallions. Stir for 30 seconds
6. Return chicken to pan. Pour in sauce and toss until it thickens and coats the chicken, about 1 minute
7. Add peanuts and green parts of scallions. Toss briefly and serve immediately

**Freeze:** Freeze without peanuts and scallion greens. Add fresh when reheating.

**Reheat:** Warm in a pan over medium heat. Add a splash of water if sauce has thickened too much.
