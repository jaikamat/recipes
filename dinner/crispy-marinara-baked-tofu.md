### Crispy Marinara Baked Tofu

**Servings:** 10 sticks
**Per serving:** 110 cal | 7g protein | 6g fat | 7g carbs
**Per 100g:** 200 cal | 13g protein | 11g fat | 13g carbs (~55g per stick)

Almond-crusted baked tofu dipped in marinara — crispy, simple, and high protein.

**Ingredients:**

_Tofu:_

- 400g (14 oz) extra firm tofu, drained

_Dredge:_

- 16g (2 TBSP) cornstarch

_Wet mixture:_

- 120g (1/2 cup) marinara sauce
- 1 egg

_Almond crust:_

- 70g almond flour
- 35g breadcrumbs
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 3/4 tsp italian seasoning
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/8 tsp MSG
- 3/4 tsp sea salt
- 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper

_For serving:_

- 120g (1/2 cup) marinara sauce for dipping

**Instructions:**

1. Press tofu in a TofuBud for 30 minutes, then slice into 10 sticks about 1/2-inch thick
2. Set up three containers: cornstarch in the first, marinara whisked with egg in the second, and almond crust mixture (almond flour, breadcrumbs, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, oregano, MSG, salt, cayenne) in the third
3. Place tofu sticks in the cornstarch container and shake to coat. Transfer to the marinara-egg mixture and shake. Then transfer to the almond crust container and shake to coat evenly
4. Lightly spray coated sticks with oil on both sides. Place in a single layer in the air fryer basket — do not overlap
5. Air fry at 375°F for 15-20 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crispy
6. Serve with remaining marinara sauce for dipping

**Storage:** Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 4 days.

**Reheat:** Air fry at 375°F for 5 minutes to re-crisp. Microwave works but the coating will soften.
