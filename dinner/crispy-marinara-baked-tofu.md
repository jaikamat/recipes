### Crispy Marinara Baked Tofu

**Servings:** 10 sticks
**Per serving:** 108 cal | 6g carbs | 7g protein | 7g fat

Almond-crusted baked tofu dipped in marinara — crispy, simple, and high protein.

**Ingredients:**

_Tofu:_
- 400g (14 oz) extra firm tofu, drained

_Almond crust:_
- 96g (1 cup) almond flour
- 17g (2 TBSP) corn meal
- 10g (2 TBSP) nutritional yeast
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp dried basil
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper

_Sauce:_
- 245g (1 cup) no-salt-added or low-sodium marinara sauce, divided

**Instructions:**

1. Press tofu in a TofuBud for 30 minutes, then slice into 10 sticks about 1/2-inch thick
3. Preheat oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with foil
4. In a shallow dish, combine almond flour, corn meal, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, basil, oregano, and cayenne pepper
5. Place half the marinara (~1/2 cup) in a bowl. Dredge tofu slices in the marinara, then press into the almond mixture to coat both sides well. Gently shake off excess
6. Lay coated slices flat on the prepared baking sheet. Bake for 40-45 minutes, flipping every 10-15 minutes, until golden and crispy
7. Serve with the remaining marinara sauce for dipping

**Storage:** Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 4 days.

**Reheat:** Oven at 400°F for 10 minutes to re-crisp. Microwave works but the coating will soften.

**Notes:** The original recipe does not specify an oven temperature — 400°F works well for a crispy crust.
