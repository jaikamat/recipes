### Butter Chicken Gravy

**Servings:** 4
**Per serving:** 294 cal | 15g carbs | 6g protein | 24g fat

A rich butter chicken gravy. Pair with chili chicken meatballs for a complete meal.

**Ingredients:**

- 56g (4 TBSP) ghee, divided
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 1 TBSP ginger paste
- 2 TBSP minced garlic
- 2-3 tsp granulated sugar
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 TBSP tomato paste
- 397g (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 300ml (1 1/4 cups) Kirkland chicken bone broth
- 1 TBSP kasoori methi, plus extra for garnish
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 5 whole cloves
- 6 green cardamom pods
- 120ml (1/2 cup) heavy cream
- 28g (2 TBSP) cold unsalted butter (optional)
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**

1. Heat half the ghee in a large pot over medium heat. Add onions, ginger, and garlic, season with salt, and cook 3-4 minutes until softened
2. Add sugar, coriander, cumin, turmeric, Kashmiri chili powder, and garam masala. Cook 30-60 seconds until fragrant. Stir in tomato paste and cook 1-2 minutes until darkened
3. Add crushed tomatoes, broth, and kasoori methi. Place cinnamon stick, cloves, and cardamom in cheesecloth, wrap tightly, and add to the sauce. Bring to a boil, then simmer 10-15 minutes until the vegetables soften and the mixture reduces by about 25%
4. Remove spice bundle. Puree the sauce with an immersion blender until smooth. Adjust consistency with broth as needed
5. Add remaining ghee and simmer for 5 minutes until slightly thickened with visible fat globules
7. Add cream and simmer 1 minute. Stir in cold butter if using. Season with salt

**Freeze:** Cool completely before freezing in containers for up to 3 months.

**Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge. Warm in a pot over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally.

**Serve:** Over basmati rice with chili chicken meatballs. Garnish with kasoori methi and fresh cilantro.

**Notes:** Kashmiri chili powder gives color with mild heat; substitute with 1 TBSP paprika + 1 tsp cayenne if unavailable. Macros listed are without the optional butter; with butter, add ~50 cal and 6g fat per serving.
