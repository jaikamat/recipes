### Butter Chicken Gravy

**Servings:** 4
**Per serving:** 229 cal | 18g carbs | 4g protein | 17g fat

A rich, creamy butter chicken gravy. Pair with chili chicken meatballs for a complete meal.

**Ingredients:**

- 500g tomatoes, roughly chopped
- 100g onions, roughly chopped
- 1 TBSP garlic paste
- 50g cashews
- 4 tsp sugar
- 2 TBSP Kashmiri chili powder
- 1/2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp kasoori methi, plus extra for garnish
- 2.5 TBSP butter, divided
- 3 TBSP Lactaid milk
- 2 TBSP malt vinegar (or 1.5 TBSP white vinegar)
- Salt to taste
- Oil for cooking

**Instructions:**

1. Heat a spoonful of butter and a splash of oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add onions and cook until softened
2. Add tomatoes and cashews. Cook until the tomatoes begin to break down
3. Add about 1 cup water, garlic paste, salt, malt vinegar, sugar, garam masala, and Kashmiri chili powder. Stir to combine and simmer for 15-20 minutes until everything is very soft
4. Transfer the mixture to a blender and puree until completely smooth
5. Strain the puree back into the pan through a fine mesh sieve, pressing to extract as much sauce as possible
6. Add remaining butter, Lactaid milk, and kasoori methi. Simmer for 5-7 minutes until the gravy thickens slightly and the flavors meld
7. Garnish with a pinch of kasoori methi

**Freeze:** Cool completely before freezing in containers for up to 3 months.

**Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge. Warm in a pot over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally.

**Serve:** Over basmati rice with chili chicken meatballs.

**Notes:** Sugar is 4 tsp, not 4 TBSP — the original recipe has a known typo. Start with 2 tsp and adjust to taste. Kashmiri chili powder gives color with mild heat; substitute with 1 TBSP paprika + 1 tsp cayenne if unavailable.
