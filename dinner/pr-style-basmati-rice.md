### PR Style Basmati Rice

**Servings:** 4 (200g each)
**Per serving:** 323 cal | 11g protein | 5g fat | 59g carbs

**Ingredients:**

- 300g basmati rice, uncooked
- 465g chicken bone broth
- 30g sofrito sauce
- 14g olive oil
- 3g salt
- 1.5g garlic powder
- 1.5g MSG
- 1.4g sazon (coriander & annatto)

**Instructions:**

1. Rinse basmati rice until water runs clear and drain
2. Add all ingredients to rice cooker and stir to combine
3. Cook on regular rice setting
4. Once finished, fluff with a fork and let rest for 5 minutes
5. Portion into 1-cup molds, pressing down lightly

**Freeze:** In four 1-cup molds overnight.

**Reheat:** Microwave for 2-3 minutes until heated through.
