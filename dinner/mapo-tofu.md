### Mapo Tofu

**Servings:** 2
**Per serving:** 516 cal | 32g protein | 30g fat | 28g carbs
**Per 100g:** 99 cal | 6g protein | 6g fat | 5g carbs (~520g per serving)

**Ingredients:**

- 400g soft tofu, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 30g ghee (high smoke point for blooming chile paste)
- 125g ground turkey (93/7)
- 40g doubanjiang (fermented bean & chile paste)
- 15g gochugaru chile powder
- 15g ginger paste
- 30g garlic paste
- 75g scallions, sliced (white and light green parts separated)

- 2g Szechuan peppercorns, ground
- 30g Shaoxing wine
- 300g (1.25 cups) Kirkland chicken bone broth
- 10g soy sauce
- 5g granulated sugar
- 3g sea salt
- 1g MSG
- 5g cornstarch, mixed with 20g water
- 5g sesame oil

**Instructions:**

1. Heat ghee in a large pan or pot over medium-high heat. Add ground turkey and cook, breaking it up, until browned
2. Add doubanjiang and gochugaru. Stir and cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant and oil turns red
3. Add ginger, garlic, and white parts of scallions. Cook for another minute
4. Pour in Shaoxing wine and let it sizzle for 30 seconds
5. Add bone broth, soy sauce, sugar, salt, and MSG if using. Bring to a simmer
6. Gently add tofu cubes. Simmer for 5-6 minutes, carefully stirring occasionally
7. Stir cornstarch slurry and drizzle into the pot. Gently stir until sauce thickens, about 1 minute
8. Remove from heat. Drizzle with sesame oil and top with Szechuan peppercorns and green parts of scallions

**Freeze:** In portions after cooling. Tofu will become chewier and more porous, absorbing more sauce.

**Reheat:** Warm gently in a pot over medium-low heat. Add a splash of broth if too thick.
