### Easy Turkey Chili

**Servings:** 4
**Per serving:** 371 cal | 32g protein | 11g fat | 36g carbs
**Per 100g:** 112 cal | 10g protein | 3g fat | 11g carbs (~330g per serving)

A quick weeknight chili using pantry staples and ground turkey. The onion and garlic go in after the meat browns and sweat in the rendered fat, which is where most of the depth comes from.

**Ingredients:**

- 454g (1 lb) ground turkey (93/7)
- 75g (about 1/2) onion, diced
- 8g garlic paste
- 1 can (15.5 oz) Bush's Chili Magic Classic Southwest Chili Starter
- 1 can (15 oz) chili beans
- 1 TBSP chili seasoning
- 2 cups spinach, chopped

**Instructions:**

1. Brown the ground turkey in a large pot over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks
2. When the meat is browned and well broken down, stir in the onion, garlic paste, and a pinch of salt, scraping up any fond stuck to the bottom. Cover and cook 5 minutes, until the onion is soft and translucent
3. Add the chili seasoning and stir to coat the meat
4. Add the Bush's Chili Magic and chili beans and stir to combine
5. Bring to a simmer and cook 10-15 minutes to let the flavors meld
6. Stir in the chopped spinach and cook until wilted, about 2 minutes

**Freeze:** Cool completely, then portion into freezer bags or containers. Freeze for up to 3 months.

**Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge. Warm in a pot over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally. Add a splash of water or broth if too thick.

**Notes:**

- Use 85/15 turkey for a richer chili — adds about 50 cal and 7g fat per serving
- The Classic Southwest starter is tomato- and pinto-based rather than the white variety's thinner great northern base, so carbs land higher and the pot reads as a red chili. Both cans are bean-forward — drop the separate can of chili beans if you want the meat to lead, which takes it to roughly 273 cal | 27g protein | 10g fat | 19g carbs per serving
- Sodium runs high, and it is almost entirely the cans. The starter is 1,000mg per 130g serving, so a whole can puts about 845mg into every portion before the chili beans or the seasoning blend add their own. Taste before adding any salt at the end — the pinch at step 2 is there to pull moisture out of the onion, not to season the pot
- 93/7 renders enough fat to sweat the onion, but add a teaspoon of olive oil at step 2 if the pot looks dry
