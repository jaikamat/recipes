### Fresh Lemon and Chile Pasta

**Servings:** 4
**Per serving:** 452 cal | 85g carbs | 30g protein | 20g fat

Andy Baraghani's creamy, bright, and spicy pasta — butter, lemon, serrano, and parmesan come together in minutes.

**Ingredients:**

- 454g (1 lb) Carbe Diem spaghetti
- 40g (3 TBSP) unsalted butter
- 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 small serrano chile, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 30g (2 TBSP) Shaoxing wine
- 1 lemon
- 120ml (1/2 cup) 2% milk
- 2-3g (1/2 tsp) sodium citrate
- 130g (4.5 oz) finely grated parmesan cheese, plus more for serving
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and season generously with salt. Cook pasta until a bit firmer than al dente, about 2 minutes less than package directions. Reserve 480ml (2 cups) pasta water, then drain
2. While the water comes to a boil, melt butter in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add garlic, chile, and pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until garlic has softened and the mixture is very fragrant, about 2 minutes. Pour in Shaoxing wine and let it cook off for 30 seconds
3. Zest the lemon directly into the pot, then pour in the milk with sodium citrate whisked in. Stir to combine, season with salt, and bring to a gentle simmer
4. Remove from heat. Gradually whisk in the parmesan until the sauce is smooth and fully emulsified. Whisk in 240ml (1 cup) of the reserved pasta water to loosen — the sauce should be slightly thinner than desired since the pasta will absorb some
5. Add the drained pasta and toss to coat. If the sauce is too thick, add more pasta water 1-2 TBSP at a time. Season with salt and pepper
6. Divide among bowls, sprinkle with more parmesan and pepper, and top with any chile slices clinging to the pot

**Serve:** Immediately while saucy — the sauce thickens as it cools.

**Notes:** Sodium citrate prevents the parmesan from clumping and splitting in a low-fat sauce. In a pinch, mix 1 tsp lemon juice with a pinch of baking soda, let it fizz out completely, and use that instead. Sauce can be made ahead without pasta and stored in the fridge for 3-4 days — reheat gently with a splash of milk or water to loosen.
