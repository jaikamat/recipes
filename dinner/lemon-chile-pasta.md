### Fresh Lemon and Chile Pasta

**Servings:** 4
**Per serving:** 452 cal | 85g carbs | 30g protein | 20g fat

Andy Baraghani's creamy, bright, and spicy pasta — butter, lemon, serrano, and parmesan come together in minutes.

**Ingredients:**

- 454g (1 lb) Carbe Diem spaghetti
- 40g (3 TBSP) unsalted butter
- 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 small serrano chile, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1 lemon
- 120ml (1/2 cup) 2% milk
- 130g (4.5 oz) finely grated parmesan cheese, plus more for serving
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and season generously with salt. Cook pasta until a bit firmer than al dente, about 2 minutes less than package directions. Reserve 480ml (2 cups) pasta water, then drain
2. While the water comes to a boil, melt butter in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add garlic, chile, and pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until garlic has softened and the mixture is very fragrant, about 2 minutes
3. Zest the lemon directly into the pot, then pour in the milk and stir to combine. Season with salt and bring to a gentle simmer
4. Add the drained pasta and 240ml (1 cup) of the reserved pasta water. Toss often with tongs while gradually sprinkling in the parmesan, 2-3 minutes, until the sauce is creamy and clings to the pasta. If the sauce looks too thick, add more pasta water 1-2 TBSP at a time
5. Turn off heat. Halve the zested lemon and squeeze the juice from one half into the pot. Taste and add more juice from the remaining half for extra tang. Season with salt and pepper
6. Divide among bowls, sprinkle with more parmesan and pepper, and top with any chile slices clinging to the pot

**Serve:** Immediately while saucy — the sauce thickens as it cools.
