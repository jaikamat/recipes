### Chili Chicken Meatballs

**Servings:** 2 (about 7 meatballs each)
**Per serving:** 417 cal | 5g carbs | 45g protein | 25g fat

Spiced Indian-style chicken meatballs with a crispy pan-fried crust.

**Ingredients:**

- 454g (1 lb) ground chicken
- 1/2 tsp cornstarch
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 1 tsp garlic paste or minced garlic
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp lemon juice
- 1 green chili, finely chopped
- 60g (1/4 cup) onion, finely chopped
- 1 TBSP cilantro, chopped
- 1/2 tsp oil (for the mixture)
- 1 TBSP oil (for cooking)

**Instructions:**

1. In a large bowl, combine ground chicken, cornstarch, salt, ginger paste, and garlic paste. Mix well until the meat becomes slightly tacky
2. Add garam masala, coriander powder, cumin seeds, lemon juice, green chilis, onion, cilantro, and 1 tsp oil. Mix again until evenly combined
3. Form into 14 meatballs
4. Heat oil in a cast iron skillet over medium heat until shimmering
5. Working in batches, add meatballs and cook without moving for 3-4 minutes until a brown crust forms on the bottom
6. Turn and continue cooking on all sides, 8-10 minutes total, until golden brown and cooked through (165°F internal)

**Freeze:** Cool completely, then freeze in a single layer on a sheet pan. Transfer to freezer bags once solid. Freeze for up to 3 months.

**Reheat:** Oven at 375°F for 10-12 minutes from frozen, or 6-8 minutes from refrigerated.

**Storage:** Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 4 days.

**Notes:** Malt vinegar can substitute for lemon juice if preferred. Adjust green chilis to taste — 2 gives moderate heat.
