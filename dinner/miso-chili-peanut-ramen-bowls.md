### Miso Chili Peanut Ramen Bowls

A brothy, miso-forward evolution of the chili peanut noodles — peanut butter powder and light coconut milk keep it lean, while lean ground turkey pushes the protein up. Chili crisp stands in for red curry paste.

**Servings:** 2
**Per serving:** 691 cal | 67g protein | 19g fat | 115g carbs
**Per 100g:** 122 cal | 12g protein | 3g fat | 20g carbs (~565g per serving)

**Ingredients:**

_Seasoned turkey crumble:_

- 227g (8 oz) lean ground turkey (99/1)
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 1 TBSP light soy sauce
- 0.5 TBSP dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp (4g) granulated sugar
- 0.5 TBSP olive oil

_Miso peanut broth:_

- 0.5 TBSP olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp ginger paste (optional — covers the aromatic lift red curry paste would give)
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 TBSP chili crisp (sub for red curry paste; add more to taste)
- 34g (2 TBSP) white miso (shiro)
- 24g (4 TBSP) peanut butter powder
- 12g (1 TBSP) granulated sugar
- 2 TBSP light soy sauce
- 10ml (2 tsp) rice vinegar
- 360ml (1.5 cups) Kirkland chicken bone broth
- 60ml (1/4 cup) light coconut milk

_Noodles & garnish:_

- 255g (9 oz) Carbe Diem spaghetti (or ~180g any ramen noodles)
- 6g (2 tsp) sesame seeds
- 33g (1/3 cup) green onion, sliced — whites for the broth, greens for garnish
- Cilantro, julienned cucumber, chopped peanuts, extra chili crisp for serving (garnish, not in macros)

**Instructions:**

1. Cook the noodles al dente in a separate pot. Reserve a splash of cooking water, then drain and rinse under cold water so they don't keep cooking
2. Brown the turkey: heat 0.5 TBSP olive oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high. Add the turkey and grated garlic, breaking it up. Once it's no longer pink, add 1 TBSP light soy, 0.5 TBSP dark soy, and 1 tsp sugar. Keep cooking 5–8 minutes until the crumbles are dry, browned, and a little crisp at the edges. Transfer to a bowl and lightly wipe the pan
3. Bloom the aromatics: in the same pan, heat the remaining 0.5 TBSP oil over medium. Add the minced garlic, ginger paste, red pepper flakes, and green onion whites; fry 30–60 seconds until fragrant. Stir in the chili crisp, peanut butter powder, sugar, and rice vinegar and work it into a thick paste, about 1 minute — don't let it scorch
4. Build the broth: pour in the Kirkland chicken bone broth, the coconut milk, and the 2 TBSP light soy. Whisk until fully smooth and the paste dissolves. Bring to a gentle simmer (not a hard boil — that turns the broth harsh) for 5–8 minutes until slightly thickened and creamy. Taste and adjust
5. Add the drained noodles to the broth and simmer 1–2 minutes to coat and warm through. Loosen with a splash of pasta water or more broth if you want it soupier
6. Take the pan off the heat. Ladle a little of the hot broth into a small bowl, whisk the miso into it until smooth, then stir it back into the pan off the heat so the miso never boils. Taste and adjust
7. Serve: ladle noodles and broth into bowls. Top with the seasoned turkey, green onion greens, sesame seeds, cilantro, cucumber, peanuts, and a spoon of chili crisp

**Storage:** The broth base and seasoned turkey both keep 3–4 days in the fridge, stored separately from the noodles. Noodles soak up broth as they sit, so add a splash of water or broth when reheating to loosen. Best assembled fresh; the dressed bowl doesn't freeze well.

**Notes:**

- **Miso:** Shiro (white) miso is the mild, slightly sweet one and the right call here. It goes in off the heat at the very end (step 6) so it never boils — a hard boil turns the flavor sharp and grainy
- **Sodium:** This uses unsalted Kirkland chicken bone broth, so both tablespoons of light soy go into the broth to season it. Miso, chili crisp, and soy still stack up, so taste before serving — you can hold back the second tablespoon of soy if you're watching sodium
- **Tofu swap (vegetarian):** Replace the turkey with 250g super-firm / high-protein tofu, grated on the large holes of a box grater and dry-fried in a nonstick pan until golden and chewy (10–15 min), then glazed with ~1 TBSP Bachan's and the grated garlic. Macros shift to roughly **760 cal | 60g protein | 28g fat | 118g carbs** per serving — the extra fat is from the soybeans, so this is the less-lean option
- **Lower fat lever:** Browning the turkey renders a little fat and the pan is nonstick, so you can drop the turkey's 0.5 TBSP oil entirely to shave ~3g fat/serving
- **Peanut flavor:** PB powder slurried into the broth matches the peanut hit of real peanut butter at a fraction of the fat. Add another tablespoon of powder for a thicker, nuttier broth rather than reaching for more oil or coconut milk
- **Coconut milk:** Leftover light coconut milk freezes well — whisk the can smooth, portion into 2 TBSP silicone cubes (this uses 2), and freeze. It separates when thawed but re-emulsifies in the warm broth; add it frozen straight to the pan
