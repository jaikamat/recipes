### Date Paste

**Yield:** ~2 cups (16 servings, 2 TBSP each)
**Per serving:** 83 cal | 0.5g protein | 0g fat | 23g carbs

A natural sweetener made from Medjool dates. Use as a 1:1 substitute for honey or maple syrup in baking, oatmeal, and smoothies.

**Ingredients:**

- 340g (12 oz) pitted Medjool dates, about 18–20
- 240mL (1 cup) reserved soaking water, plus more for desired consistency

**Instructions:**

1. Bring 4 cups of water to a simmer and remove from heat
2. Place dates into a large glass measuring cup and cover with the hot water. Let sit for 10 minutes to soften
3. Reserve 360mL (1.5 cups) of the soaking liquid, then drain the remaining water from the dates
4. Transfer dates and 240mL (1 cup) of the reserved water to a high-speed blender. Blend until smooth, adding more reserved water for a thinner consistency
5. Transfer to a glass container and cover

**Freeze:** Portion into 2 TBSP servings in a silicone mold or ice cube tray. Flash freeze, then transfer to a freezer bag. Keeps 3–4 months.

**Storage:** Refrigerate in a covered glass container for up to 3 months.
