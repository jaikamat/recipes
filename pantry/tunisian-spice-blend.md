### Tunisian Spice Blend

**Yield:** ~9 tbsp (using 1 tsp per part)

A warm, aromatic North African spice blend. Great on roasted vegetables, grilled meats, or stirred into soups and stews.

**Ingredients:**

- 4 parts ground caraway seed
- 4 parts ground coriander
- 4 parts smoked paprika
- 4 parts turmeric
- 4 parts chili powder
- 4 parts garlic powder
- 1 part cayenne pepper
- 1 part cinnamon
- 1 part ground black pepper

**Instructions:**

1. Combine all ingredients in a small bowl and whisk to combine.
2. Transfer to an airtight container or jar.

**Storage:** Store in a cool, dark place for up to 6 months.

**Notes:** Scale using any measure as your "part" — 1 tsp per part yields about 9 tbsp, 1 tbsp per part yields about 1.5 cups.
