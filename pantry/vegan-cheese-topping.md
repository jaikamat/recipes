### Vegan Vegetable "Cheese" Topping

**Yield:** ~1/4 cup

A nutty, savory nutritional-yeast blend with MSG for a cheesy, umami hit. Sprinkle over roasted or steamed vegetables.

**Ingredients:**

- 3 TBSP nutritional yeast
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/8 tsp MSG
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes

**Instructions:**

1. Combine all ingredients in a small bowl and whisk to combine
2. Sprinkle over desired vegetables

**Storage:** Store in an airtight container in a cool, dark place for up to 6 months.
