### Aaji's Garam Masala

**Yield:** ~1.5-2 cups

A robust North Indian garam masala from grandmother's recipe. Heavy on coriander, peppercorn, and cloves for warmth and punch. Black cumin and black mace set it apart from store-bought versions. It's over 150 years old!

**Ingredients:**

- 75g (1/2 cup) coriander seeds
- 50g (6 TBSP) whole cloves
- 50g (1/2 cup) black peppercorns
- 40g (4 TBSP) brown cardamom seeds
- 40g cinnamon sticks, snapped into small pieces
- 30g (3 TBSP) cumin seeds
- 15g (2 TBSP) grated nutmeg
- 15g (1.5 TBSP) black mace
- 4 bay leaves

**Instructions:**

1. Dry-toast all spices except the grated nutmeg in a skillet over medium heat for 3-5 minutes until fragrant, stirring frequently so nothing burns
2. Let the toasted spices cool completely
3. Break up the bay leaves by hand and snap cinnamon sticks into smaller pieces if not already done
4. Add all toasted spices and the nutmeg to a Vitamix and grind to a fine powder
5. Transfer to an airtight jar

**Storage:** Store in an airtight jar away from light for up to 6 months.

**Notes:** If using whole brown cardamom pods, crack them open and use the seeds, discarding the outer husks — unless you prefer a smokier flavor, in which case grind them whole. This is a large batch meant for keeping and gifting.
