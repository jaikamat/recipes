### Tiramisu Overnight Oats

**Servings:** 1
**Per serving:** 355 cal | 29g protein | 3g fat | 56g carbs

A layered tiramisu take on overnight oats — an espresso-soaked oat base under a blended cottage cheese "mascarpone" cream, dusted with cocoa at serving. No cooking required and no caffeine.

**Ingredients:**

_Espresso oat layer:_

- 20g (1/4 cup) rolled oats
- 1.5 frozen date paste cubes (3 TBSP)
- 1.5 tsp decaf espresso powder
- 30mL (2 TBSP) Fairlife 2% ultra-filtered milk
- Pinch of sea salt

_Cream layer:_

- 115g (1/2 cup) 0% cottage cheese
- 30mL (2 TBSP) Fairlife 2% ultra-filtered milk
- 10g vanilla whey protein
- 1mL (1/4 tsp) vanilla extract
- 5g (1 tsp) erythritol

_Topping:_

- 2g (1 tsp) dutch process cocoa powder

**Instructions:**

1. Blend cottage cheese, milk, whey protein, vanilla, and erythritol in a high-sided container using an immersion blender until smooth. Vacuum out bubbles if possible
2. Stir together date paste cube, espresso powder, salt, and milk in the serving container until the espresso dissolves, then mix in the rolled oats
3. Spoon the cream layer gently over the oat layer without mixing — it should sit on top like the mascarpone layer of a tiramisu
4. Seal and refrigerate overnight or at least 4 hours

**Serve:** Dust the cocoa powder over the top just before eating so it stays dry. Eat cold, scooping down through both layers.

**Storage:** Keeps in the fridge for up to a week. Hold the cocoa dusting until serving.

**Notes:**

- Use regular instant espresso powder if caffeine isn't a concern (~60mg per tsp)
- A few drops of rum extract in the oat layer nods to the marsala in classic tiramisu
- Try 5g chia seeds in the oat layer for a thicker, more ladyfinger-like base (~25 cal)
- Shave a little dark chocolate over the cocoa dusting for a fancier finish
- Everything can also be shaken together MUSH-style like the dark chocolate version if you'd rather skip the layering
