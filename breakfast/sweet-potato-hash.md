### Sweet Potato Hash

**Servings:** 6
**Per serving:** 118 cal | 2g protein | 5g fat | 18g carbs
**Per 100g:** 91 cal | 2g protein | 4g fat | 14g carbs (~130g per serving)

**Ingredients:**

- 3 medium sweet potatoes
- 2 poblano peppers
- 1 large onion
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp smokey chipotle powder or bbq spice
- 2 tsp salt
- ~2 TBSP olive oil

**Instructions:**

1. Peel and dice sweet potato into small pieces, about 1 inch cubes
2. Wash and dice pepper and onion to be slightly larger than the sweet potato since they will cook faster
3. Put cut veggies on a sheet tray, season with olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt and pepper and mix well to make sure everything is thoroughly coated
4. Place into a 425°F oven for 20 minutes then flip the tray and cook for another 7-10 minutes or until the veggies are slightly softened and start to brown on the edge
5. Let cool completely, then place the hash into 1 cup molds filling all the way to the top, pressing down lightly

**Freeze:** In six 1/2 cup molds overnight.

**Reheat:** Microwave for 5-7 minutes or until fully defrosted.
