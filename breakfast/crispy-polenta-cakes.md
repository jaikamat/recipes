### Crispy Polenta Cakes

**Servings:** 6
**Per serving:** 319 cal | 15g protein | 17g fat | 25g carbs

**Ingredients:**

- ½ onion
- 1 box of crimini mushrooms
- ½ lb bacon
- 1 bag of spinach
- 1 cup of polenta
- 3 cups water (For creamier: add 6 cups water, or sub ½ the water with milk)

**Instructions:**

1. Dice onion, slice mushrooms, and cut bacon into 1 inch squares
2. In a small pan, add bacon on low heat and begin to render the fat, stirring occasionally. Once bacon is crispy and brown, remove from pan and strain the fat. Put both aside
3. Turn pan up to medium high heat and add sliced mushrooms and a pinch of salt. Let all the water evaporate out, about 5-6 minutes, then put aside as well
4. In a medium sized pot, add in half your bacon fat and the onions and cook until translucent. Add back mushrooms and bacon and stir
5. Add polenta to the pot and toast slightly. Add water and mix well. Immediately add spinach and continuously stir for 10-15 minutes or until the polenta thickens and the grains slightly change to a lighter color yellow
6. Pour the mixture into your molds, filling all the way to the top and let set. Make sure it is completely cooled before covering

**Freeze:** In six 1/2 cup molds overnight after completely cooled.

**Reheat:** Air fryer for 10 minutes at 375°F, then flip and fry for another 5 minutes. For creamier option: microwave for 7 minutes (add more liquid if too stiff).
