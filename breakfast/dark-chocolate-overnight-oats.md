### Dark Chocolate Overnight Oats

**Servings:** 1
**Per serving:** 335 cal | 23g protein | 7g fat | 54g carbs

A MUSH-style overnight oats with dark cocoa and date paste — dairy- and soy-free using Ripple pea milk and Truvani protein. No cooking required — just blend, mix, and refrigerate overnight.

**Ingredients:**

- 1.5 frozen date paste cubes (3 TBSP)
- 180ml (3/4 cup) Ripple unsweetened original pea milk
- 17g Truvani unflavored protein powder (≈12g protein)
- 10g (2 TBSP) dutch process cocoa powder
- 20g (1/4 cup) rolled oats
- 1/8 tsp xanthan gum
- 1mL (1/4 tsp) vanilla extract
- 5g (1 tsp) erythritol
- Pinch of sea salt

**Instructions:**

1. Blend Ripple, Truvani protein powder, and xanthan gum in a high-sided container with an immersion blender until completely smooth — disperse the powder into the liquid now, since adding it dry later makes the oats gummy and clumpy. Let the foam settle or vacuum out bubbles if possible
2. Combine date paste cube, cocoa powder, vanilla, erythritol, salt, and half the Ripple mixture in a container. Seal and shake until smooth
3. Add the remaining Ripple mixture and rolled oats. Shake to combine
4. Refrigerate overnight or at least 4 hours

**Serve:** Stir well before eating. If it set up firmer than you like (the protein powder and xanthan keep thickening overnight), loosen with a splash of Ripple. Enjoy cold straight from the fridge.

**Storage:** Keeps in the fridge for up to a week.

**Notes:**

- Unflavored pea protein can read slightly earthy — the cocoa and date paste cover it; bump the erythritol if you want it sweeter
- Keep the xanthan light (1/8 tsp or less) — the Truvani already thickens the oats overnight, and too much xanthan turns it gluey
- For a leaner version closer to the original 4g fat, swap ~60ml of the Ripple for water and nudge the Truvani up to ~19g to hold the protein — it costs a little creaminess
- Try espresso powder (1/4 tsp) to deepen chocolate flavor without adding coffee taste
- Natural (non-alkalized) cocoa works too, for a sharper, more acidic chocolate
- Try a pinch of cardamom for a floral, exotic warmth that pairs with dates
- Try fresh orange zest for a classic French chocolate pairing
- Try a tiny pinch of cayenne for Mexican hot chocolate warmth
- Try flaky finishing salt (Maldon) on top at serving instead of mixing salt in for texture contrast
