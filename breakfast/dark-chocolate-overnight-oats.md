### Dark Chocolate Overnight Oats

**Servings:** 1
**Per serving:** 280 cal | 20g protein | 5g fat | 46g carbs

A MUSH-style overnight oats with dark cocoa and date paste. No cooking required — just mix and refrigerate overnight.

**Ingredients:**

- 1 frozen date paste cube (2 TBSP)
- 115g (1/2 cup) 1% cottage cheese
- 10g (2 TBSP) cocoa powder (natural, unsweetened)
- 120mL (1/2 cup) unsweetened vanilla almond milk
- 20g (1/4 cup) rolled oats
- Pinch of salt

**Instructions:**

1. Blend cottage cheese and almond milk until smooth
2. Combine date paste cube, cocoa powder, salt, and half the milk mixture in a container. Seal and shake
3. Add the remaining milk mixture and rolled oats. Shake to combine
4. Refrigerate overnight or at least 4 hours

**Serve:** Stir well before eating. Enjoy cold straight from the fridge.

**Storage:** Keeps in the fridge for up to a week.
