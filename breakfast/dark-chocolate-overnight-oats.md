### Dark Chocolate Overnight Oats

**Servings:** 1
**Per serving:** 336 cal | 23g protein | 7g fat | 53g carbs

A MUSH-style overnight oats with dark cocoa and whole dates — dairy- and soy-free using Ripple pea milk and Truvani protein. No cooking required — blend everything smooth, pour over the oats, and refrigerate overnight.

**Ingredients:**

- 2 Medjool dates, pitted
- 180ml (3/4 cup) Ripple unsweetened original pea milk
- 17g Truvani unflavored protein powder (≈12g protein)
- 10g (2 TBSP) dutch process cocoa powder
- 1/4 tsp decaf espresso powder
- 20g (1/4 cup) rolled oats
- 1/8 tsp xanthan gum
- 1mL (1/4 tsp) vanilla extract
- 7g (1 tsp) erythritol
- Pinch of sea salt

**Instructions:**

1. Add the dates, Ripple, cocoa powder, espresso powder, vanilla, erythritol, and salt to a blender. Blend on high until completely smooth, about 1 minute — the dates should fully break down with no bits remaining
2. Add the Truvani protein powder and xanthan gum and blend on low just until incorporated, a few seconds — adding them after the dates are pulverized keeps the mix from trapping and holding air
3. Put the rolled oats in a jar or container and pour the blended mixture over them. Stir to combine, then tap on the counter to release air bubbles
4. Refrigerate overnight or at least 4 hours

**Serve:** Stir well before eating. If it set up firmer than you like (the protein powder and xanthan keep thickening overnight), loosen with a splash of Ripple. Enjoy cold straight from the fridge.

**Storage:** Keeps in the fridge for up to a week.

**Notes:**

- If your dates are dry or firm, or you're not using a high-speed blender, soak them in warm water for 10 minutes and drain before blending so they purée smoothly
- The decaf espresso powder deepens the chocolate without adding coffee flavor — it's worth keeping in
- Unflavored pea protein can read slightly earthy — the cocoa, dates, and espresso cover it; bump the erythritol if you want it sweeter
- Keep the xanthan light (1/8 tsp or less) — the Truvani already thickens the oats overnight, and too much xanthan turns it gluey
- For a leaner version closer to 4g fat, swap ~60ml of the Ripple for water and nudge the Truvani up to ~19g to hold the protein — it costs a little creaminess
- Natural (non-alkalized) cocoa works too, for a sharper, more acidic chocolate
- Try a pinch of cardamom for a floral, exotic warmth that pairs with dates
- Try fresh orange zest for a classic French chocolate pairing
- Try a tiny pinch of cayenne for Mexican hot chocolate warmth
- Try flaky finishing salt (Maldon) on top at serving instead of mixing salt in for texture contrast
