### Dark Chocolate Overnight Oats

**Servings:** 1
**Per serving:** 327 cal | 23g protein | 4g fat | 58g carbs

A MUSH-style overnight oats with dark cocoa and date paste. No cooking required — just mix and refrigerate overnight.

**Ingredients:**

- 1.5 frozen date paste cubes (3 TBSP)
- 115g (1/2 cup) 1% cottage cheese
- 10g (2 TBSP) cocoa powder (natural, unsweetened)
- 60mL (1/4 cup) Fairlife 0% skim milk
- 20g (1/4 cup) rolled oats
- 1mL (1/4 tsp) vanilla extract
- 5g (1 tsp) erythritol
- Pinch of salt

**Instructions:**

1. Blend cottage cheese and milk in a high-sided container using an immersion blender until smooth. Vacuum out bubbles if possible
2. Combine date paste cube, cocoa powder, vanilla, erythritol, salt, and half the milk mixture in a container. Seal and shake
3. Add the remaining milk mixture and rolled oats. Shake to combine
4. Refrigerate overnight or at least 4 hours

**Serve:** Stir well before eating. Enjoy cold straight from the fridge.

**Storage:** Keeps in the fridge for up to a week.

**Notes:**

- Try espresso powder (1/4 tsp) to deepen chocolate flavor without adding coffee taste
- Try dutch process cocoa instead of natural for a smoother, less acidic chocolate
- Try 5g chia seeds for a thicker pudding-like mouthfeel (~25 cal)
- Try 1-2g white miso paste for umami depth — a pastry chef trick that makes chocolate taste richer
- Try a pinch of cardamom for a floral, exotic warmth that pairs with dates
- Try fresh orange zest for a classic French chocolate pairing
- Try a tiny pinch of cayenne for Mexican hot chocolate warmth
- Try flaky finishing salt (Maldon) on top at serving instead of mixing salt in for texture contrast
