### Dark Chocolate Overnight Oats

**Servings:** 1
**Per serving:** 305 cal | 23g protein | 9g fat | 47g carbs

A MUSH-style overnight oats with dark cocoa and a whole date — dairy- and soy-free using Ripple pea milk and Orgain plant protein. No cooking required — blend everything smooth, pour over the oats, and refrigerate overnight.

**Ingredients:**

- 1 Medjool date, pitted
- 180ml (3/4 cup) Ripple unsweetened original pea milk
- 29g Orgain vanilla protein powder (≈12g protein)
- 10g (2 TBSP) dutch process cocoa powder
- 1/4 tsp decaf espresso powder
- 20g (1/4 cup) rolled oats
- 1mL (1/4 tsp) vanilla extract
- 5g (1 tsp) erythritol
- Pinch of sea salt

**Instructions:**

1. Add the date, Ripple, cocoa powder, espresso powder, vanilla, erythritol, and salt to a blender. Blend on high until completely smooth, about 1 minute — the date should fully break down with no bits remaining
2. Add the Orgain protein powder and blend on low just until incorporated, a few seconds — adding it after the date is pulverized keeps the mix from trapping and holding air
3. Put the rolled oats in a jar or container and pour the blended mixture over them. Stir to combine, then tap on the counter to release air bubbles
4. Refrigerate overnight or at least 4 hours

**Serve:** Stir well before eating. If it set up firmer than you like (the protein powder and its gums keep thickening overnight), loosen with a splash of Ripple. Enjoy cold straight from the fridge.

**Storage:** Keeps in the fridge for up to a week.

**Notes:**

- If your date is dry or firm, or you're not using a high-speed blender, soak it in warm water for 10 minutes and drain before blending so it purées smoothly
- The decaf espresso powder deepens the chocolate without adding coffee flavor — it's worth keeping in
- With only one date, the erythritol does real work — between the bitter dutch cocoa and espresso, the Orgain's stevia alone won't carry it. Start at 1 tsp and scale up toward 2 tsp for a dessert-sweet cup, or back off if you like it darker
- Orgain contains guar and xanthan gum, so it thickens the oats on its own overnight — no added thickener needed
- Orgain is fairly salty (~220mg sodium per 29g serving), so go light on the added pinch of salt or leave it out
- Swapping to Orgain trades macros for taste: it brings ~30 more calories and ~11g more carbs (with ~4g extra fiber) per serving than the leaner Truvani, but holds the same ~12g protein from the powder
- For a sweeter, more caramel-forward version, add a second Medjool date (adds ~16g carbs and ~60 cal) — the original build used two
- Natural (non-alkalized) cocoa works too, for a sharper, more acidic chocolate
- Try a pinch of cardamom for a floral, exotic warmth that pairs with dates
- Try fresh orange zest for a classic French chocolate pairing
- Try a tiny pinch of cayenne for Mexican hot chocolate warmth
- Try flaky finishing salt (Maldon) on top at serving instead of mixing salt in for texture contrast
