### Sausage, Egg, and Cheese Savory Baked Oatmeal

**Servings:** 6
**Per serving:** 577 cal | 42g protein | 27g fat | 39g carbs
**Per 100g:** 199 cal | 14g protein | 9g fat | 13g carbs (~290g per serving)

A savory, high-protein baked oatmeal with homemade breakfast sausage, peppers, and cheddar.

**Ingredients:**

_Sausage:_
- 1 batch breakfast sausage (see recipe), uncooked
- 1 TBSP oil

_Oatmeal:_
- 240g (3 cups) rolled oats
- 40g (1/2 cup) oat flour
- 6 eggs
- 480ml (2 cups) Fairlife 2% ultra-filtered milk
- 226g (1 cup) 0% cottage cheese
- 113g (1 cup) cheddar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1 poblano pepper, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 TBSP oil

**Instructions:**

1. Prepare one batch of breakfast sausage mixture (do not mold)
2. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the sausage, breaking it into small pieces until fully cooked. Remove and set aside
3. In the same skillet, add oil and sauté diced onion and poblano pepper until lightly colored
4. Preheat oven to 375°F
5. Beat eggs in a large bowl and combine with milk and cottage cheese
6. Mix in oats, oat flour, baking powder, salt, cooked sausage, sautéed peppers and onion, and shredded cheddar
7. Spread mixture evenly in a greased 13x9" pan
8. Bake for 40-45 minutes until set in the center
9. Let cool slightly, then cut into 6 equal portions

**Freeze:** Cool completely, then wrap portions individually or store in freezer bags for up to 3 months.

**Reheat:** From frozen, microwave at 50% power for 3-4 minutes. Oven at 350°F for 15-20 minutes also works.
