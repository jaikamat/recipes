# Ricotta Pancakes

**Per serving:** 268 cal | 24g carbs | 12g protein | 13g fat

**Ingredients:**

- 120g flour
- 5g baking powder
- 1.25g baking soda
- 1.5g salt
- 185g ricotta cheese
- 90g milk or water
- 100g eggs (2 large)
- 2.5g vanilla extract
- 28g butter, melted

**Instructions:**

1. In a large bowl, combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt
1. In a separate bowl or blender, whisk ricotta, milk, eggs, vanilla, and melted butter until smooth
1. Pour wet mixture into flour bowl and whisk until just combined with no visible flour
1. Heat a large non-stick pan or griddle over medium heat and grease once hot
1. Pour about 60g of batter per pancake, gently spreading if needed
1. Cook for about 2 minutes until edges look set and bubbles form
1. Flip once and cook another 1-2 minutes on the other side
1. Don’t press down with the spatula and only flip once

**Makes:** 4 servings

**Serve:** With butter, maple syrup, or your favorite toppings​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
